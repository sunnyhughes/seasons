# seasons
Seasons of the Sun is a personal website that I created to tell a little bit of my life story. I wanted to give a glimpse into the life of a teenage parent who through many obstacles, managed to raise 3 exceptional Men. With help of a present and active father, their paternal grandmother, a few best friends, and a large group of people who made up my "village", I made a dent in the world of troubles and split the head of many doubters. I succeeded in obtaining an education, a loving home, and a great family despite the challenges that any other teenage unwed mother of 3 would have succumbed to. I am no different than any other mother. I wanted what was best for my children and I fought HARD to provide for them. This site is just a small testament of my love for them. My love for my family.
